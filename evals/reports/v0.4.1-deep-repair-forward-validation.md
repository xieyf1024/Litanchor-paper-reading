## What this validation proves

The three former blind-test papers were prepared again from Zotero and rebuilt with richer evidence/claim ledgers. All three:

- use `Paper Template - Final.md`;
- retain only the verified first author in note frontmatter;
- keep the complete SourceBundle author list;
- pass deterministic quote, physical-page, numeric and visual-provenance checks;
- contain multiple verified Zotero page targets rather than a fixed `p.1`;
- pass template-completeness, content-recall and section-depth gates.

The warnings are the already-visible multi-column/ligature/rotated-text extraction notices. They do not bypass the quality gate and require review before Obsidian export.

## Regression contrast

The invalid earlier notes contained only 11–12 claims and used a simplified hard-coded outline. The new previews are not accepted merely because they are longer: the build is blocked unless required content groups, explanatory detail, source-proportional coverage, Final-template headings, traceable quotes and verified pages all pass.

## Remaining limitation

`prepare_pdf()` still creates empty ledgers. The three forward-validation ledgers were produced through the documented specialised reading passes, but there is not yet a self-contained automatic model runner inside `litanchor_local.py`. Therefore this validates:

1. the richer intermediate contract;
2. Final-template rendering;
3. deterministic quality blocking;
4. real-paper compatibility.

It does **not** yet prove unattended full-paper extraction. Promotion beyond the v0.4 repair remains blocked on a repeatable extraction/orchestration path and human comparison against the three silver references.
