# v0.6 public-beta validation

Date: 2026-07-29
Candidate: `v0.6.0-beta.1`

## Decision

**PASS for the public-beta release gate.** Three release-eligible frozen
holdouts passed the rubric without a paper-specific change to the
distributable Skill. The notes remain reviewable candidates rather than
automatically accepted user notes.

## Holdout integrity

The first frozen round exposed two general failures. Those papers were moved
to the development set before the fixes were made. One proposed replacement
was disqualified before semantic evaluation because the same PDF hash had
appeared in an older private run. It was replaced rather than counted.

The final release decision therefore uses:

| Case | Route | Pages | Evidence | Claims | Result |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `holdout-rumaila-toc` | whole document | 12 | 26 | 22 | pass |
| `holdout-phanerozoic-deep-learning` | selected complex pages | 17 | 43 | 27 | pass |
| `holdout-model-interpretability` | whole document | 10 | 33 | 29 | pass |

All three runs started without reference notes or prefilled ledgers. Their
fidelity review, recall review, Final-template completeness, evidence
coverage, page accuracy, numeric fidelity and Markdown checks passed.

## MinerU fusion

Across the three holdouts, MinerU returned 265 blocks. Of those, 236 aligned
to a PyMuPDF physical page (`89.06%`), added two section candidates and twelve
figure candidates, and created zero authoritative EvidenceUnits.

The long-document route selected original pages 4, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 without
renumbering them. The two eligible documents used the whole-document route.
This demonstrates that MinerU is an automated conditional enhancement, while
PyMuPDF remains the page, quotation, crop and Zotero-link authority.

## Pathological PDF suite

Six synthetic cases passed in the project virtual environment:

- two-column reading order;
- image-only/blank-page fallback;
- mixed readable and blank pages;
- more than 60 physical pages;
- substantive Methods after References;
- MinerU timeout with intact PyMuPDF fallback.

No copyrighted PDF page is tracked by these fixtures.

## Remaining human boundary

The deterministic release gate does not silently accept notes into a user's
knowledge base. Visual/link acceptance and collision decisions remain user
review boundaries. This is intentional and does not block the Agent-installed
public beta.
