# v0.6 MinerU component A/B

Date: 2026-07-29

## Comparison

The A/B boundary is component-level:

```text
PyMuPDF baseline
vs.
the same PyMuPDF baseline plus aligned MinerU structure hints
```

PyMuPDF-only establishes physical pages, text blocks, coordinates, quotations,
Zotero links and original-PDF crops. Fusion may add headings, reading-order
hints and figure candidates, but it cannot create authoritative evidence.

| Case | Route | Baseline sections | Added sections | Added figure candidates | Aligned / all blocks |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Rumaila TOC | whole document | 8 | 1 | 0 | 128 / 133 |
| Phanerozoic deep learning | selected pages | 8 | 0 | 7 | 18 / 18 |
| Model interpretability | whole document | 10 | 1 | 5 | 90 / 114 |

## Finding

Fusion improved at least one target structural measure on all three release
cases: it added section boundaries on two papers and added twelve figure
candidates across the long-subset and eligible whole-document routes. Evidence
fidelity did not regress because MinerU produced zero authoritative
EvidenceUnits and all final claims returned to verified PyMuPDF pages.

The report does not claim that every MinerU block is more accurate than native
PDF text. Twenty-nine blocks remained unmatched, and aligned formula/table
text is still treated as a candidate requiring original-page verification.

## Failure behavior

The synthetic timeout fixture confirms that an unavailable MinerU service is
recorded as an optional enhancement failure and the run continues on the
unchanged PyMuPDF baseline. Consent remains local and the service is not called
when the configured mode forbids upload.
