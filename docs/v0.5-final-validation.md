# LitAnchor v0.5 final validation candidate

Date: 2026-07-26
Branch: `codex/v0.5-autonomous-deep-reading`
Candidate: `0.5.0-rc1`

## Scope decision

The release gate uses six autonomous papers: three calibration papers and three
previously unseen blind papers. ResNet, LOVECLIM and U-Net are retained as an
extended renderer/quality-gate regression because their v0.4.1 ledgers were
human-assisted. Re-running those three and then calling all nine “autonomous”
would inflate the evaluation and blur the anti-leak boundary.

## What v0.5 now proves

For one paper obtained from Zotero, the candidate can:

1. build a PyMuPDF-authoritative physical-page package;
2. classify paper type and execute six explicit full-text reading passes;
3. fuse consented MinerU structure without treating it as evidence;
4. materialize page-verified EvidenceUnits before ClaimRecords;
5. synthesize complete Final-template sections rather than one-line placeholders;
6. evaluate every figure, select 1–3 key method/result visuals and validate crops;
7. run independent fidelity and paper-type recall reviews;
8. block incomplete summaries, page audits, numerical traces or visual contracts;
9. export only after review, path containment and no-overwrite checks.

## Additional generalization smoke test

Three papers not used by the official six-paper calibration/evaluation set were
processed with the same frozen workflow. They cover social-science empirical
research, a geology method paper and a long AI-research benchmark paper.

| Paper | Primary type | Pages | Evidence | Claims | Visuals | Result |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Perceived Barriers for Accessing International Research Funding among Latin American Researchers | empirical | 11 | 26 | 24 | 2 | completed with warnings and user reviewed |
| Synthetic Geology: Structural Geology Meets Deep Learning | method/algorithm | 20 | 52 | 35 | 3 | completed with warnings and user reviewed |
| ResearchGym: Evaluating Language Model Agents on Real-World AI Research | empirical, with benchmark/method secondary profiles | 71 | 52 | 31 | 2 | completed with warnings and user reviewed |

The test exposed paper-independent improvements rather than paper-specific
answers: complete method-flow recall, separate equation/metric/parameter claim
types, original-page verification receipts for damaged scientific symbols,
primary plus secondary paper profiles, multi-evidence support for combined
claims, and idempotent promotion of warning-state notes after user review. The
three notes were regenerated from their structured artifacts after these
changes; the final Markdown was not hand-edited.
