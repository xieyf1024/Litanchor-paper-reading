# Reading method

This optional reference distills the project author's reading workflow and
early note-template experience. Treat it as a heuristic for selecting reading
depth and composing the learning layer, not as a paper-fact source, external
authority or reliability rule.

## Select depth

- Use `skim` to decide relevance quickly. Cover the title, abstract, structure,
  question, method overview, main result, bounded conclusion and whether deeper
  reading is worthwhile. Render only template Section 1.
- Use `deep` for a research-grade understanding of one paper. Cover the complete
  argument, data/materials, methods, equations/metrics, core experiments,
  experiment-to-claim evidence chains, results, selected visuals, author
  interpretation, conclusion boundaries and author-stated limitations. Render
  template Sections 1–6 and omit the learning layer.
- Use `internalize` when the user wants to transfer the paper into research
  practice. Include everything in `deep`, then add source-labelled learning,
  writing, visualization, knowledge-connection and testable research-idea work
  in template Sections 7–8.

## Three passes

1. **Skeleton:** read title, abstract, headings, conclusion and the final introduction paragraphs. Identify paper type, theme, method and contribution.
2. **Close reading:** inspect introduction, methods, metrics, results, discussion, key visuals, limitations and unresolved terminology. Extract evidence while reading.
3. **Reconstruction:** restate the research logic and identify what the user may learn from design, method, visuals and writing.

## Output emphasis

- Abstract: purpose, method, result, conclusion.
- Introduction: background, prior work, unresolved gap, purpose and claimed contribution.
- Methods: object/data/materials, sample, model, steps, parameters, tools and evaluation criteria.
- Results: reported observations and numbers.
- Discussion: author explanations, comparison with prior work and uncertainty.
- Conclusion: main findings, limitations and future directions actually stated by the authors.

## 125 learning layer

For `internalize`, add:

- one research/method/project idea that passes `research-idea-gates.md`;
- two figures worth studying for communication design;
- five English expressions or writing patterns from the paper.

Label the entire layer: `以下属于学习启发，不是作者原文结论。` Mark
source-grounded interpretation as `[分析]`, the research idea as `[假设]`, and
user-supplied connections as `[用户]`. Do not present a suggested idea as an
author claim. Preserve exact evidence for quoted expressions.

## Questions and snowballing

List unresolved terms/questions, potentially citable passages and references worth following. State only why the current paper makes them relevant; do not retrieve or summarize external sources in the closed-source note.
