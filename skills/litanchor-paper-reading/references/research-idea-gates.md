# Research-idea gates

Use these gates only for `internalize`. Research ideas are source-grounded Agent
hypotheses, not paper claims and not verified novelty.

## Required fields

1. **Source observation:** tie the idea to a verified limitation, failure case,
   assumption, contradiction or unexplained result.
2. **Falsifiable hypothesis:** state a proposition that could be wrong.
3. **Delta:** state what changes relative to the paper: representation,
   mechanism, data, objective, feedback, evaluation or deployment constraint.
4. **Validation:** specify comparison, controlled variables, data or population,
   metric, expected observation and falsifying result.
5. **Failure modes:** give at least two plausible reasons the idea may fail.
6. **Novelty status:** use `unverified`, `partially_checked` or
   `prior_art_checked`.

## Language boundary

Use `候选想法`, `假设`, `可能扩展` or `需要检索现有工作`. Do not call an idea
novel, first or unexplored unless a separate cited prior-art search was performed.
Formal LitAnchor notes remain source-closed, so the default novelty status is
`unverified`.

## Completion criterion

Reject a candidate that lacks a source observation, falsifiable hypothesis,
specific delta, executable validation or two failure modes. Keep rejected ideas
out of the note instead of filling the template with vague suggestions.
